MaintenanceApp Project Title : Student Accomodation Maintenance System

Project description: The Student Accommodation Maintenance Management System is a software solution designed to improve the way maintenance problems are reported,
managed, and tracked within student accommodation.
Currently, students report maintenance issues through WhatsApp messages, phone calls, and face-to-face communication, making it difficult for landlords or accommodation managers to keep track of requests and determine which problems have been resolved or are still outstanding.
Students also have limited visibility into the progress of their reported issues.
The proposed system will provide a centralised platform where students can submit maintenance requests,
provide details about the problem, and monitor the status of their requests.
Accommodation managers or landlords will be able to view, prioritise, assign, update,
and manage maintenance requests from one location. The system aims to improve communication,
accountability, response times, and record-keeping, while providing students with a simple and transparent way to monitor their maintenance issues.
